FAQ

Blog and guide questions

Why read the guides before starting a quote?

They help you compare products, understand cost drivers and prepare the details that shape the final estimate.

Do these guides replace the quote tool?

No. They support the quote tool by helping you make clearer product and scope decisions before submission.

Can I still get a quote if I am unsure which product I want?

Yes. Use the guides to compare the main options, then start the quote with the product path that looks closest.

Can I measure the area before reading the guides?

Yes. The floor plan tool is available if you need to confirm the floor area before choosing the final quote inputs.
