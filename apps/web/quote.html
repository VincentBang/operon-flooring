        Common questions

        Do I need exact measurements to start?

        No. Start with the best area you know now, even if it is only an approximate total or an early floor plan measurement. The quote is designed to give you a structured starting estimate first, then confirm final measurements before the work is locked in.

        Can I upload a floor plan instead of measuring manually?

        Yes. The floor plan tool lets you trace the flooring rooms, calculate a measured area, and send that figure back into the quote flow. It is a practical option when you do not want to measure each room by hand, but final scope items still need review in the quote itself.

        Is the online quote the final price?

        No. The online result is a structured estimate, not the final approved contract price. Final price is confirmed after the selected product, area, scope items and relevant site details are reviewed together.

        Can I choose supply and install or installation only?

        Yes. You can use the quote for both supply and install projects or installation-only work where the flooring product is already chosen or supplied separately. The flow adjusts the estimate path so the pricing structure matches the type of job you are planning.

        Am I committed when I send the quote?

        No. Sending the estimate does not lock you in or force a booking. It simply gives us the project details in one structured place so the scope can be reviewed properly and the next step can be confirmed with you.
