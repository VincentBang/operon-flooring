        Common questions

        Do I need exact measurements to start?

        No. Start with the best area you know now. Final measurements can be checked before the quote is confirmed.

        Can I upload a floor plan instead of measuring manually?

        Yes. Use the floor plan tool to trace rooms, confirm area, and send that measured total into the quote.

        Is the online quote the final price?

        No. The online result is an estimate. Final pricing is confirmed after measurements, site details and any extra scope are checked.

        Can I choose supply and install or installation only?

        Yes. The quote wizard supports both supply and install, and installation-only projects.

        Am I committed when I send the quote?

        No. Sending the estimate does not lock you in. It gives us the project details so we can review and confirm the next step with you.
