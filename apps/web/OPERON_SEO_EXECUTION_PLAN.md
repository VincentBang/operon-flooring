## Part 2 — Page Content Structure Template

All target pages should follow this structure unless there is a strong reason not to.

### Core structure

1. H1
2. Intro with keyword and Sydney / suburb context
3. Cost discussion
4. Product explanation
5. Use cases
6. Comparison section
7. CTA to quote
8. FAQ section
9. Internal links

### Content rules

- no fluff
- no filler sections just to increase length
- no keyword stuffing
- no vague “AI” language
- practical trade-informed tone
- use real customer decision questions
- move users toward quote or floor plan measurement

### CTA rules

Every page should include at least one strong CTA:

- `Start quote`
- `Measure from floor plan`
- `Open quote tool`

## Part 4 — Backlink Acquisition Plan

Goal:

- 20–50 backlinks in the first 60–90 days

Important:

- Do not chase random low-quality links.
- Start with structured local trust signals and supplier relevance.
- Keep NAP consistent everywhere.

### Step 1 — Local Citations

Priority citation list:

- Google Business Profile
- Yelp
- TrueLocal
- Hotfrog
- Oneflare
- ServiceSeeking
- Yellow Pages
- LocalSearch
- Word of Mouth

Action:

- standardise business name, address and phone
- make business description consistent
- link each listing back to homepage or the main quote page
- use category wording aligned with flooring installation, laminate, hybrid and timber flooring

### Step 2 — Trade Directories

Targets:

- flooring directories
- renovation directories
- construction directories
- interior fitout directories
- home improvement directories

Execution rule:

- prioritise directories that allow a business description, service area and website link
- avoid spam directories that provide no trust value

### Step 3 — Supplier Links

Targets:

- flooring suppliers
- wholesalers
- manufacturers

Action:

- contact suppliers and ask to be listed as an installer / partner / service provider
- use the website and location pages as credibility support
- where possible, request a direct followed website link

### Step 4 — Blog Outreach

Targets:

- Australian renovation blogs
- property blogs
- home improvement blogs
- DIY blogs

Pitch angles:

- `flooring cost guide`
- `laminate vs hybrid comparison`
- `how to measure floor area from a floor plan`
- `apartment flooring installation considerations`

### Step 5 — Local Partners

Potential partners:

- builders
- real estate agents
- property managers
- buyer's agents
- renovation consultants

Exchange options:

- referral link
- trade partner listing
- supplier / installer recommendation
- suburb resource mention

### Backlink KPI

First 60–90 day target:

- 10–15 citation links
- 5–10 relevant trade / supplier links
- 5–10 partner or outreach links

## Part 5 — How To Beat Hipages / Directory Competitors

Do not try to beat large directories with raw authority first.

Beat them with:

1. better intent match
2. tool advantage
3. local trust
4. more useful cost content
5. suburb precision

### Strategy 1 — Better Intent Match

Competitor weakness:

- generic directory pages
- too much choice friction
- poor continuity between search intent and action

Operon advantage:

- quote tool at the top
- stronger next action
- clearer conversion flow

Action:

- keep the quote wizard high on the page
- reduce friction
- keep CTAs direct
- maintain one primary action per page

### Strategy 2 — Tool Differentiation

Operon advantage:

- floor plan measurement tool
- measurement system
- structured quote workflow

Action:

- keep reinforcing:
  - `measure your floor area`
  - `upload a floor plan`
  - `clear estimate before site visit`

### Strategy 3 — Content Depth

Competitor weakness:

- thin landing pages
- generic category copy

Operon advantage:

- cost-focused practical explanations
- better product comparisons
- better area-measurement guidance

### Strategy 4 — Local Pages

Competitor weakness:

- generic city pages
- shallow suburb relevance

Operon advantage:

- suburb-level landing pages
- access and property-condition context
- location-specific trust

### Strategy 5 — Data Advantage

Long-term moat:

- quote submission data
- measurement data
- product demand signals
- suburb-level enquiry patterns

Action:

- keep improving pages based on actual quote inputs and lead data
- use customer questions from submissions to build future content
