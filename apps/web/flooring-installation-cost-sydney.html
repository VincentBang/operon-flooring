Cost Factors

What affects flooring installation cost?

Flooring cost usually depends on total area, room layout, floor preparation, removal of existing flooring, stairs, moisture barrier, skirting, scotia and product type. Simple open areas are usually easier to price and install than complex layouts with many small rooms, transitions or awkward details.

That is why the quote system asks for real project conditions instead of relying on a rough per-square-metre guess alone.

Product Types

Laminate, hybrid and engineered timber

Different flooring categories affect cost in different ways. Laminate is usually the more budget-conscious option, hybrid is popular where durability and water resistance matter, and engineered timber sits higher for customers who want a more natural timber finish.
