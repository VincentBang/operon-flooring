FAQ

Hybrid flooring Sydney questions

Is hybrid flooring waterproof?

Most modern hybrid flooring uses a waterproof core, which is why it is commonly chosen for kitchens, living areas and busy family homes. That said, waterproof does not mean every installation detail can be ignored, so the selected range and site conditions still need to suit the project properly.

Is hybrid flooring suitable for apartments?

Hybrid flooring is often suitable for apartments because it is commonly installed as a floating floor and gives a practical, low-maintenance finish. Acoustic rules, building requirements and the specific product setup still need to be checked before the final installation path is confirmed.

How much does hybrid flooring cost in Sydney?

Hybrid flooring cost depends on the selected range, measured area and scope items such as removal, trims, moisture protection or stairs. The quote flow gives you a structured starting estimate first, then the final scope is confirmed before booking.

Does hybrid flooring need underlay?

Some hybrid ranges already include an attached acoustic layer, while other projects may still need a separate underlay depending on the product and installation setup. It is worth checking this early because underlay affects comfort, sound performance and the overall scope of the quote.

Can hybrid flooring be installed over tiles?

Sometimes, yes, but only when the existing tile surface is sound, level enough and suitable for the selected product system. If the tiles are uneven, loose or causing height issues, preparation may still be needed before installation is confirmed.

Is hybrid better than laminate flooring?

Hybrid is usually the stronger option when water resistance, durability and everyday practicality matter most. Laminate can still be the better value-led option for dry internal rooms where the brief is simpler and the project does not need the same moisture-focused performance.

How long does hybrid flooring installation take?

Installation time depends on the area, room layout, removal, preparation needs and how clear the site scope is before work starts. A smaller straightforward job may move quickly, while projects with stairs, old floor removal or subfloor work naturally take longer.
