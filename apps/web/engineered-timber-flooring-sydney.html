FAQ

Engineered timber flooring Sydney questions

What is engineered timber flooring?

Engineered timber flooring uses a real timber wear layer over a layered base. It is usually chosen when the natural timber finish is central to the renovation result.

How much does engineered timber flooring cost in Sydney?

Cost depends on the selected range, measured area, installation method, pattern, preparation and finishing scope. The quote flow gives a starting estimate before final confirmation.

Can engineered timber be installed as floating or direct glue?

Straight-plank engineered timber can often be assessed as floating or direct glue. Herringbone and chevron installations require direct glue and clearer site-scope review.

Is engineered timber better than hybrid flooring?

Engineered timber is usually better when real timber appearance is the priority. Hybrid is often better when water resistance, low maintenance and a simpler project path matter more.

Can engineered timber be used in apartments?

It can be suitable, but acoustic rules, building requirements and the installation method need to be checked before an apartment project is confirmed.

Can I use the floor plan tool for an engineered timber quote?

Yes. The floor plan tool can be used to confirm the area before returning to the quote flow.
