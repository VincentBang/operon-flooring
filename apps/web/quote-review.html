FAQ

Common quote review questions

What does a complete flooring quote include?

A complete flooring quote clearly lists product, installation method, site preparation, trims, removal, disposal and site details before final confirmation.

Why can flooring quotes change?

Quotes often change when product details, subfloor condition, moisture requirements or finishing items are not fully confirmed at the start.

Should I accept a flooring quote without checking scope?

It is better to confirm the product, installation method, preparation, finishing items and site details first. A total only makes sense when the included work is clear.

Should removal and disposal be listed separately?

Yes. Removal and disposal are commonly separate. A quote can include lifting old flooring without including take-away disposal.
