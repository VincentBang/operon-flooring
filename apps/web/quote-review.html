Scope checklist

What a complete flooring quote should show

A useful written quote should make the product, area basis and main inclusions clear before you compare totals. Look for product detail, measured area, GST status, underlay, removal, finishing details, stairs, exclusions and any items still subject to final confirmation.

FAQ

Common quote review questions

What does a complete flooring quote include?

A complete flooring quote should clearly list the product range, installation method, area basis, main inclusions, finishing details and any items still subject to final project confirmation. The goal is to understand what work is included before you decide.

Why can flooring quotes look different?

Flooring quotes can look different when the product, area basis, main inclusions or finishing details are written differently. Compare what is included before comparing totals.

Should I accept a flooring quote without checking inclusions?

It is better to confirm the product, installation method, main inclusions, finishing items and final project details first. A total is easier to compare when the included work and open items are clearly written.

Should removal and disposal be listed separately?

Yes. Removal and disposal are often separate items, and one can be included without the other. A quote may allow for lifting the old floor but still leave take-away disposal, dumping fees or extra labour to be confirmed later.

Can this review turn into an Operon estimate?

Yes. If the review identifies the flooring type, area and enough scope detail, you can use it as a starting point for a structured Operon estimate. If product range, preparation, finishing items or final project details are unclear, the safer next step is to confirm those items before comparing totals.
