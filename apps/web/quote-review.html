FAQ

Common quote review questions

What does a complete flooring quote include?

A complete flooring quote should clearly list the product range, installation method, area basis, removal, disposal, floor preparation, trims or scotia, stairs and any items still subject to site confirmation. The goal is not just to see a total, but to understand exactly what work is included before you compare prices.

Why can flooring quotes change?

Flooring quotes usually change when important details are not fully confirmed at the start, such as product selection, subfloor condition, moisture protection, trims, stairs or preparation. Most quote differences come from missing or unclear scope, not just from the headline rate.

Should I accept a flooring quote without checking scope?

It is better to confirm the product, installation method, preparation, finishing items and site details first. A total only becomes meaningful when the included work, excluded work and items still needing site confirmation are clearly written.

Should removal and disposal be listed separately?

Yes. Removal and disposal are often separate items, and one can be included without the other. A quote may allow for lifting the old floor but still leave take-away disposal, dumping fees or extra labour to be confirmed later.
