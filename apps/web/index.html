When is a floor plan useful?

A floor plan is useful when room measurements are unclear, the layout is awkward, or you want a faster starting area without measuring each room manually. The tool lets you trace flooring spaces and send the measured area into the quote page.

What should I check before accepting a flooring quote?

Check the product range, area basis, main inclusions, finishing details, warranty and exclusions. If anything is unclear, ask for it in writing.

Why can two flooring quotes look different?
