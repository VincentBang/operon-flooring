How To Use It

Simple measurement flow

1. Upload floor plan Use a PDF, JPG or PNG.
2. Set scale Click two points on a known wall length, then enter the real distance.
3. Trace room Trace around the room. Return near the start point to close it automatically.
4. Use area in quote Select the flooring rooms only, then send the total area into your quote.
