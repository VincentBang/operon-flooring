    Floor Plan Measurement FAQ

    Before you use the measured area

    Is this the final flooring measurement?

    No. It gives a measured starting area from your floor plan. Final details can still be confirmed before installation.

    What kind of floor plan works best?

    A PDF, JPG or PNG with visible room walls and at least one known dimension.

    Does this calculate the final quote?

    No. It sends measured area into the quote. Product choice, main inclusions and final project details are reviewed separately.

    Should I include every room?

    Only trace rooms where flooring will be installed.
