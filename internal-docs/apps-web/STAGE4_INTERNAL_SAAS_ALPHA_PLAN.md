# Stage 4 Internal SaaS Alpha Plan

Date: 2026-06-04

Purpose: define the internal-only Operon OS alpha before any contractor-facing SaaS or marketplace work.

## Goal

Use Operon OS internally across Flooring first, then Kitchens, before selling or exposing it externally.

## Alpha Users

- Operon operator/admin
- Future internal sales/admin helper

Not included:

- Customers
- Contractors
- Marketplace partners
- Public SaaS users

## Alpha Capabilities

- Lead list
- Lead detail
- Status pipeline
- Notes
- Follow-up queue
- Quote-review queue
- Floorplan review queue
- Reporting dashboard
- Source/event timeline

## Success Criteria

- No leads missed from quote/contact/review/upload sources.
- Follow-ups are visible and actionable.
- Operator can identify next action within 30 seconds.
- Uploaded files are safely represented.
- No internal pricing exposed to public browser.
- Flooring quote flow remains stable.

## Non-Goals

- No subscriptions.
- No contractor accounts.
- No customer portal.
- No automated dispatch.
- No payment processing.

## Alpha Exit Gate

Internal SaaS alpha can graduate only after:

- 2-4 weeks of stable internal use.
- Lead source coverage is verified.
- Follow-up queue is useful.
- Security audit passes.
- Pricing/privacy leak tests pass.
