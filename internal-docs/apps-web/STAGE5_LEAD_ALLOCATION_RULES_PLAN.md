# Stage 5 Lead Allocation Rules Plan

Date: 2026-06-04

Purpose: define manual-first lead allocation before contractor dispatch tooling exists.

## Allocation Inputs

- Suburb/zone
- Product category
- Job value
- Complexity
- Floorplan/quote-review status
- Customer urgency
- Contractor capacity
- Contractor fit score

## Manual-First Rule

The system may recommend a contractor, but an Operon operator approves allocation manually.

## Recommended Scoring Factors

- Service area match
- Product capability
- Response reliability
- Quality score
- Current capacity
- Similar project proof
- Customer communication fit

## Blockers

- Missing insurance/licence.
- Contractor inactive.
- Capacity unavailable.
- High complaint/rework risk.
- Job outside approved scope.

## Non-Goals

- No automatic customer assignment.
- No bidding marketplace.
- No automated price negotiation.
- No public contractor ranking.

## Required Data Before Automation

- Enough closed jobs to validate quality.
- Response-time records.
- Complaint/rework records.
- Quote acceptance data.
- Operator override reasons.
