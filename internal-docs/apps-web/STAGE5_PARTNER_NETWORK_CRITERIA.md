# Stage 5 Partner Network Criteria

Date: 2026-06-04

Purpose: define minimum standards before any controlled contractor network is considered.

## Entry Criteria

Contractors must provide:

- Licence or relevant trade registration where applicable.
- Public liability insurance.
- Workers compensation where applicable.
- Recent project proof.
- Product/category capability.
- Service suburbs.
- Warranty expectations.
- Communication standard acceptance.

## Quality Requirements

- Clear scope confirmation before job starts.
- Photo proof before/after where appropriate.
- Defect/rework process.
- Clean handover notes.
- Respect for customer property.

## Response Standard

- Acknowledge lead within agreed response window.
- Confirm site measure availability.
- Update Operon when quote sent.
- Update Operon when won/lost.

## Exclusion Triggers

- Missing insurance.
- Repeated poor communication.
- Defect/rework pattern.
- Unapproved pricing changes after scope confirmation.
- Customer complaints without resolution.

## Non-Goals

- No open marketplace.
- No paid lead marketplace yet.
- No customer-facing contractor browsing.
- No automatic dispatch.

## Gate

Do not start partner onboarding until Stage 3 and Stage 4 internal operations are stable.
