# Operon Floor Care & Maintenance Content Pillar

## 1. Pillar Objective

Build a floor care and maintenance content cluster that attracts Sydney customers who:

- already have laminate, hybrid, or engineered timber floors
- are trying to solve maintenance or damage concerns
- are comparing flooring types before choosing replacement
- may need a repair-or-replacement quote soon

The pillar should support:

- SEO traffic
- product education
- trust
- quote conversion
- long-term topical authority

## 2. Target Audience

Primary audiences:

- homeowners maintaining existing floors
- renovators comparing replacement options
- apartment owners dealing with lifting, swelling, scratches, or moisture concerns
- landlords and property managers looking for practical flooring decisions
- customers who want to know whether to repair or replace flooring

## 9. Content Quality Rules

- Keep tone practical, calm, and trade-informed
- Avoid generic filler
- Avoid alarmist wording
- Keep paragraphs short and readable
- Use clear H2 structure
- Include replacement and quote pathways where genuinely helpful
- Do not make unsupported performance or warranty claims

## 10. Maintenance Disclaimer Rules

Use wording like:

- `may`
- `can`
- `often`
- `depends on product and installation`
- `check manufacturer care instructions`
- `final assessment may require inspection`

Do not claim:

- all hybrid is fully waterproof in all situations
- damaged laminate can always be repaired
- engineered timber can always be sanded
- warranty always covers swelling or lifting
