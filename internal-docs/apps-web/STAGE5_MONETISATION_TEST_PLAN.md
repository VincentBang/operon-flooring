# Stage 5 Monetisation Test Plan

Date: 2026-06-04

Purpose: compare monetisation models only after operations data is visible.

## Models To Compare

- Referral fee
- Managed job margin
- Subscription
- Premium lead access

## Required Inputs

- Lead volume
- Lead quality
- Quote acceptance rate
- Job value
- Contractor response rate
- Customer satisfaction
- Rework/complaint cost
- Operator time per lead

## Evaluation Criteria

- Customer trust
- Contractor fairness
- Operon margin
- Operational complexity
- Legal/compliance risk
- Quality-control burden

## Initial Recommendation

Stay manual and internal until unit economics are visible. Do not choose a monetisation model before controlled contractor alpha data exists.

## Non-Goals

- No payment flow now.
- No marketplace launch now.
- No subscription billing now.
- No public contractor lead sales now.
