# Stage 5 Customer Protection Process

Date: 2026-06-04

Purpose: define customer protection before any controlled contractor network launches.

## Scope Clarity

- Confirm product/category.
- Confirm area/measurement method.
- Confirm inclusions/exclusions.
- Confirm removal/disposal.
- Confirm stairs/trims/skirting.
- Confirm site/access constraints.

## Before Job Starts

- Customer receives clear scope.
- Contractor confirms scope.
- Warranty/process expectations are documented.
- Photos or site notes captured where useful.

## Complaint Process

1. Capture issue.
2. Request photos/details.
3. Contact contractor.
4. Classify urgency.
5. Agree remediation path.
6. Record outcome.

## Quality Control

- Completion photo proof.
- Customer feedback.
- Defect/rework tracking.
- Contractor scorecard update.

## Non-Goals

- No broad public guarantee before operations can support it.
- No automated dispute resolution.
- No unverified contractor claims.
