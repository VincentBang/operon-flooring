# Operon SEO Content Refresh Register

Source-of-truth status: working register for refreshing existing pages before creating unnecessary new pages.

## Refresh Priority Rules

Prioritize pages that already have:

- commercial intent
- internal links
- visible CTAs
- product or quote pathways
- crawl history
- likely user demand

Refresh before creating new pages when the existing asset can better satisfy the same intent.

## Operon Scope Standard Refresh Rule

Every refresh should ask whether the page helps the customer compare flooring work by scope, not price alone.

Use the internal Scope Standard to improve:

- product definition
- area and measurement clarity
- installation scope
- site and access context
- preparation and risk clarity
- finishing and accessory detail
- commercial clarity
- exclusions and assumptions
- final confirmation language

Do not force the term "Operon Scope Standard" into public page copy. Use plain phrases such as "know what is included before you decide" and "compare flooring quotes by scope, not just total price."
