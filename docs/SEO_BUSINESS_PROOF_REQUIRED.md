# Operon Flooring SEO Business Proof Required

Status: evidence required before adding stronger trust claims or structured data

The current local sprint does not add ratings, reviews, licences, public addresses, team claims, compliance claims or guaranteed performance claims. Supply the evidence below before any such statement is approved for customer-facing copy or schema.

## Business identity

- Approved legal business name and trading name
- ABN or ACN, if the business wants it published
- Verified public phone number
- Verified public email address
- Verified public service address only if customers may rely on it
- Approved service-area wording
- Approved opening hours

## Trade and insurance proof

- Relevant licence details, including the exact entity and expiry/status evidence
- Current public liability insurance evidence
- Any trade membership the business wants to claim, with a verifiable public source
- Approved wording defining whether Operon supplies, installs, coordinates or refers work

## Product and installation claims

- Manufacturer or supplier documents supporting product specifications
- Approved warranty documents and the party responsible for each warranty
- Acoustic test reports tied to the exact product/system being discussed
- Written installation-method guidance where a technical claim is proposed
- Evidence for waterproof, water-resistant, scratch-resistant or durability claims

Product evidence does not replace checking the requirements of a specific property, strata scheme or installation site.

## Project proof

For each published project claim:

- permission to use the photos;
- confirmed suburb or a decision to describe only the wider area;
- property type, if known and approved;
- flooring category and range, if known and approved;
- preparation and installation details that can be verified;
- confirmation that before/after wording matches the actual project.

Unknown details should be omitted rather than inferred.

## Reviews and ratings

- Public review-platform URL
- Current review count and average rating captured on the implementation date
- Permission and eligibility to reproduce review excerpts
- Evidence that any structured-data use follows the platform and search-engine rules

Do not add aggregate ratings or review schema until eligibility and visible-page requirements are confirmed.

## LocalBusiness and Organization schema approval

Before extending schema, approve:

- exact public name;
- canonical website URL;
- public logo asset;
- public phone and email;
- address or service-area representation;
- opening hours;
- social profile URLs;
- licence or identifier fields, if used.

## Specialist review dependencies

Obtain appropriate review before publishing statements that could be read as:

- legal or strata advice;
- guaranteed acoustic compliance;
- universal building requirements;
- guaranteed installation outcomes;
- a binding final quotation;
- warranty coverage beyond the approved written terms.

Until verified, customer copy should direct users to check their building requirements and confirm final site scope before booking.
